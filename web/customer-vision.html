Vision & mission

What we build, why, and what you should expect.

Vision

Every residential builder should be able to plan, price, picture, and permit a job in one place — without software built for every discipline on earth.

Revit’s workflows are worth keeping. Revit’s full cost and complexity are not what we sell. The tool should feel familiar on day one, use your firm’s standards, and never invent a fact it does not know.

Mission

Draw the job, price it, show the client what it will look like, and produce the compliance documents the municipality asks for — from your Revit template, not a generic CAD palette.

If a feature moves a builder toward one of those four outcomes, it belongs in the product. The only question is when, not whether.

What you get

Plan

Storey-based drawing that staff already trained on Revit can pick up.

Price

Quantities and cost from the same job — not a spreadsheet that drifts.

Picture

A client-facing view. Schematic or AI-assisted; if AI, it is marked as such.

Permit

A submission-ready set for a registered professional to check and sign. Direct submit only where registration is not required.

Exports (DXF, IFC, permit set) go out one way. The live job stays on the server.

How we work

  1. Familiar behaviour, not a second Revit. Levels, sheets, libraries, snap, groups — the interactions you already know. Not family authoring, MEP routing, or photoreal rendering.
  2. Your template is the default. Wall types, doors, and the rest come from your template. Where it runs out, we ask. We do not invent.
  3. Unknown stays unknown. Gaps show up in the report. We never fill them with a plausible number.

What we don’t pretend to be

  • Family authoring or parametric family internals
  • MEP routing (we place symbols and price PC sums)
  • Structural calculations or engineer sign-off
  • Photoreal “as-built” images
  • Automatic municipal approval, or submission without a registered professional where SACAP requires one

If something is on the roadmap, we say when it lands — not that it is out of scope forever.

The promise

We respect how you already build, we surface gaps instead of hiding them, and we stay on mission: plan, price, picture, permit. Nothing more, nothing less.
