Adaptive ribbon

One ribbon, no scrollbar. It starts at the best version (comfortable, large buttons for one- and two-command groups) and gives up the least important things first, one step at a time, until the row fits. Drag the width slider and watch the table below: low-priority groups step down first and lead the others by a couple of steps; Walls, Floors, Openings and Ceilings are protected and stay big the longest. Comfort (row height, padding) goes before button size, and dropdowns are the last resort.
