    What changed from the current panel

    • The ruler is labelled. Every tick carries the level name and height. When levels sit close together (eaves and ceiling are 218 mm apart), the labels spread out and a leader line points back to the tick.
    • One control, not two. Hovering a label previews the cut in the drawing, and hovering the drawing highlights the matching label. The duplicate list below the drawing is gone.
    • The handle looks draggable. It's a filled copper grip with a resize cursor, and it snaps to levels.
    • The view type is plain text ("Ceiling plan"), not a chip that looks like a button.
    • The isolate toggle states its scope: it acts on the plan, and the note says what "off" does. While it is on, the section fades whatever that cut leaves off the plan. The labels and the cut line stay at full strength.
    • Floor build-up has an owner. It sits under "Ground floor slab". Focusing it outlines the slab in the section, and the screed layer appears as you type. When it's empty, the note explains the consequence instead of showing "not stated" as if it were a value.
    • Names and numbers are consistent. Names are spelled out ("Ground floor", not "GFL"), the level code is a muted prefix, the unit is stated once in the column header, and negative heights use a true minus sign.
    • The break line is grey, so it reads as "the drawing continues" rather than as construction.
