How Revit behaves, and what this copies

  • Select a wall, door, window, floor or roof and a green tab opens on its own. A room does not get one: Delete is already on Modify, and the name stays in the properties.
  • Click Walls and the green tab becomes Modify | Place Wall. The location line, and the note that Space flips the face, sit on the options bar under the ribbon. Esc stops placing before it clears a selection.
  • Move keeps you on the selection tab and puts Disjoin on the options bar. Align, Join and the trims stay available with nothing selected, because they ask you to pick as you go.
  • Flip face sits with Edit this face and Second screen. It switches which side of the wall you are looking at. It does not move the wall.
  • The tags mark where each action lives today. They are part of this mock, not the ribbon to build.
