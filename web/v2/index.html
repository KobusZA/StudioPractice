      About StudioPractice

      StudioPractice

      A plan, a price, a picture, and a permit

      Version Part 3 preview

      Platform Web application
      Behaviours Revit, without the engines
      License Proprietary

      StudioPractice helps a builder get to four things: a plan, a price, a picture, and a permit. Draw the job, price it, show the client what it will look like, and produce the compliance documentation the municipality asks for. Types, levels, sheets and title blocks come from the firm’s Revit template. Unknown facts stay unknown — they are asked for, never guessed.

      Plan: storeys, walls, openings, snapping
      Price: live takeoff from the drawing
      Picture: 3D massing for the client
      Permit: sheets, title block, Check
