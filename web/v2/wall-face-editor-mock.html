How this works

1 · One pictureThis face is the editor. The inspector has no second thumbnail.

2 · Named selectionAdd a finish sits beside this box. The type and where it goes stay inside it.

3 · On the shapeDragging a corner or an edge measures the gap to the wall: left or right, and up or down. Click the same spot again to edit the finish underneath.

4 · Finishes on one shapeA second finish on the same area is a line just inside, and a “2 finishes” mark. A clash is still the hatch.

5 · Not the sheetDocument → Views → Elevation is still the compass view of the building.
