Services alongside and inside walls Mock Plan view, 1 m = 200 px. Pipe drawn at true diameter: 110 mm core, 20 mm insulation, 150 mm outer.

Four states of one idea: a route can sit beside a wall, be placed by a typed clearance, be justified to a wall face, or run inside the wall as a deliberate chase. Drag the blue pipes and nodes; type in the highlighted fields.

1Alongside: node to wall (today)

Drag the end node. The live gap is measured from the node centre to the wall face, the same dimension a wall shows while it is drawn.

Bare pipe, centreline measurement.

2Alongside: edge clearance, typed

The gap is measured from the pipe's outer edge (insulation included) to the wall face. Drag the pipe, or type a clearance and press Enter.

3Justification to the wall face

Draw direction is left to right. The reference is the wall's exterior face. The pipe keeps a constant offset to it for the whole run.

4Inside the wall: a deliberate chase

Drag the pipe into the wall. It is flagged as an obstruction until it is marked as a chase, and a chase must hold the whole pipe.

Reading the plan. Hatched band: 200 mm wall, exterior face on top. Blue: pipe core. Pale blue ring: insulation. Dashed blue-grey: live gap dimension (the same style the wall tool uses). Copper dashed: reference line. Red: obstruction. Green: accepted chase.
