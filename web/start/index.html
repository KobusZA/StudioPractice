Getting started

A first look, on your own firm. Draw one room, see what an hour costs against a fee, and add a person. About five minutes.

Open the drawing app

Draw

1. Create the firm

On the sign-in screen, choose Create an account. Enter an email, a password of at least 10 characters, and a firm name. That creates the firm and signs you in. You are the owner.

2. Start a drawing

You land on Your drawings. Press New drawing. The plan opens empty, with the ribbon along the top and Saved in the status line.

3. Draw one room

On the Draw ribbon, pick Walls. Click one corner, then the next, and keep going until you click the first corner again. Press Escape when the room is closed. Wait until the status line says Saved, then reload the page. The room is still there.

Cost

Practice operations uses the same email and password. This is where a job gets a fee, and where time is priced.

Open practice

4. Open a job that already has a fee

Press New job. Give it a code, choose Rezoning, and choose Fixed fee. Rezoning copies a fee schedule of R 74 500. The overview shows that figure as Quoted, and Captured at R 0 until somebody logs time.

5. Log an hour

Open the Time tab. Enter 60 minutes and a description. Leave pricing on the quarter-hour ladder. Before you save, the line under the form shows what the hour is worth: R 1 920, and the burn against the fee.

6. See it against the fee

Press Log it, then go back to Overview. Captured is now R 1 920, which is 2.6% of the fee. The warning says the hour has been captured and nothing has been certified yet.

People

7. Add someone

Open Team, then People, then Add team member. Enter their email, a password of at least 10 characters, and a name. Leave the role as Employee. There is no email invite: you give them the password yourself.

An employee can open jobs and log time. Fees, billed amounts, and this Team page stay with the owner. The grid on the form shows that before you press Add.

When something on these screens does not match what you see, that is the thing to report.
