# Vision & mission

A short statement of what we build, why it exists, and what you can expect when you work with us.

## Vision

Every residential builder should reach **a plan, a price, a picture, and a permit** from one place—without carrying the weight of software built for every discipline on earth.

We believe the workflows builders already know from Revit are right; the cost and complexity of full Revit are not. Our vision is a tool that feels familiar on day one, respects your firm’s standards, and never trades accuracy for speed by guessing what it does not know.

## Mission

We help your team **draw the job, price it, show the client what it will look like, and produce the compliance documentation your municipality expects**—using your template as the starting point, not a generic CAD palette.

That is our scope test: if it moves you closer to one of those four outcomes, it belongs in the product.

## What you get

| Outcome | What it means for you |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Plan** | Storey-based design with levels, underlays, attach, snap, groups, mirror, align—behaviours your Revit-trained staff already understand. |
| **Price** | Quantities and pricing tied to the job you drew, not a separate spreadsheet that drifts out of sync. |
| **Picture** | Client-facing visuals that are clear and honest—schematic or AI-assisted, with AI outputs marked as such. |
| **Permit** | Submission-ready documentation for a registered professional to review and sign; direct submission only where registration is not required. |

Deliverables such as DXF, IFC, and permit sets leave the app as **exports**. Your live job stays in one place so everyone works from the same source of truth.

## How we work with your firm

**Familiar behaviour, not a second Revit.** We keep the interactions builders expect—levels, sheets, title blocks, libraries of jobs—and leave out family authoring, MEP routing, structural sign-off engines, and photoreal rendering that most residential firms never use.

**Your template defines the defaults.** Wall types, doors, windows, fittings, levels, and sheets come from *your* Revit template. Where the template does not cover something, we ask your firm to supply or confirm it—we do not invent a stand-in.

**Unknown stays unknown.** Blank fields get filled by facts you provide or by explicit checks in our compliance workflow—not by plausible defaults that look correct until they are built wrong. Gaps show up in reports so you can fix them before they become costly mistakes.

## What we do not pretend to be

To stay focused and trustworthy, we do not offer:

- Parametric family authoring or deep 3D solid modelling
- Pipe, duct, or cable routing (we place and count service symbols; MEP is priced as PC sums)
- Structural or engineering calculations that require a registered engineer’s signature
- Photoreal rendering marketed as as-built reality
- Automated municipal approval or SACAP work without a registered professional in the loop

When something is planned for a later release, we say **when** it lands—not “out of scope” in a way that sounds like a permanent no.

## The promise

You get software that respects how your firm already builds, surfaces what is still missing instead of hiding it, and keeps its energy on **plan, price, picture, permit**—nothing more, nothing less.
